Reducing confusion when requesting vehicle insurance for Ceylinco VIP

Timeline3 days
PlatformWeb
FocusReducing confusion

The problem

The start seems normal, right? Well, yes. So here I have to fill out my brother’s details...?

That is exactly what I did: entered his name and NIC number as beneficiary and set the relationship as insured.

The external details were all filled, and I had to upload a copy of my brother’s ID and the vehicle registration certificate.

So I got his ID, front and back, and the vehicle registration licence image. Done. The rest was simple: when to activate it, how I want my policy certificate and which language.

Submitted the form, paid the amount, all was good. Until it was not. I got this confirmation email.

Then I had to call to fix the name. Apparently there was also a problem with the uploaded vehicle registration: what they wanted was the pink slip, not the revenue licence.

The solution

If this were a system I designed, here is how I would reduce the confusion. I identified three ways:

  1. Add helper text for important details
  2. Use proper terms for fields
  3. Remove unwanted fields from the form

Proper terms remove the guesswork about what to upload.

Afterword
