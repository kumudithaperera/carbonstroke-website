Logo and brand guidelines for YEZ Packaging

ClientYEZ Packaging
DeliverablesLogo, favicon, letterhead
FocusBrand guidelines

From sketches

The logo started on paper. The early sketches explored letterforms built from bottles, caps and containers, the things YEZ Packaging actually makes.

To digital concepts

The strongest sketches were redrawn digitally and tested in black, red and on grey backgrounds before narrowing down.

Brand guidelines

The final logo ships with an eight-page guideline so anyone producing packaging or print can use it correctly. It covers:

  • Primary logo and a bottle-shaped favicon
  • Clear space of one quarter of the logo width, and a minimum size of 20 × 20 mm
  • Don’ts: no redrawing, recolouring or changing the placement of its parts
  • Colour palette: black #121212 and red #DC3532, with CMYK values for print
  • An inverted version for dark backgrounds
  • Brand fonts: Poppins as primary, Palanquin as secondary
