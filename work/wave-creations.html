A logo and letterhead for Wave Creations

ClientWave Creations (Pvt) Ltd
IndustryEvent management
DeliverablesLogo, letterhead

The client

Wave Creations is a one-stop event management service based in Moratuwa, Sri Lanka. They run corporate events, college festivals, weddings, launches and more, with DJ music, decorations and photography under one roof.

The mark

The logo combines a sound wave with the W of “Wave”, so the music side of the business reads at a glance. The wordmark sits inside a circle that works as a badge on flyers, social posts and stage branding.

Letterhead

The letterhead carries the mark and the company details in the header and a red band at the footer, so printed quotations and letters match the rest of the brand.
