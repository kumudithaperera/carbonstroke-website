Save it once. Cook it forever.

Paste a link from Instagram, TikTok or any recipe blog. Morsel pulls out the ingredients, steps and macros, and keeps them on your phone.

  • Works fully offline, no account needed
  • Macros estimated for every recipe
  • Step-by-step cook mode with timers
