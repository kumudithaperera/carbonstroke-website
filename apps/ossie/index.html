Ossie asks instead of guessing.

A note that could belong in three places goes to the Inbox with Ossie’s best guesses. One tap files it. A swipe does too.

  • Your choice sticks. Move a note yourself and Ossie locks it there. It won’t move it again until you say so.
  • Every move has Undo. A banner tells you where the note went, with Undo right there.
  • Nothing happens quietly. Activity lists every automatic change from the last 30 days.

Your notes live on your phone. So does the filing.

There’s no Ossie account and no Ossie server. We never see your notes, because they never leave your device to reach us.

With Apple Intelligence

Filing, titles and tags run on the on-device model. Nothing is sent anywhere.

With your own AI key

On other phones, paste a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or another provider. Notes go straight from your phone to them, never through us.

With no AI at all

Writing, voice, transcripts, search and manual filing all still work. New notes wait in the Inbox with simple suggestions.

Fair questions.

Something else? Write to us at carbonstroke@gmail.com.

What if Ossie files a note in the wrong place?

Tap Undo on the banner, or move it yourself. A note you move stays put: Ossie locks it to that folder.

Can I make my own folders?

Yes. You and Ossie share the same folder tree. Ossie suggests new folders when a topic keeps coming up.

Which phones will it run on?

iPhone with iOS 26 and Android. Phones with Apple Intelligence file notes fully on device; others can add an AI key.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Writing, recording, transcribing, searching and on-device filing all work offline.

Who sees my notes?

Only you. If you add an AI key, the provider you chose receives the text it needs to file a note. We never do.

When can I try it?

Ossie is in development. Join the waitlist and we’ll send one email when the beta opens.
